# LaunchDock — The Modern Glassmorphic App Launcher for Windows

Transform your desktop workflow with **LaunchDock**, a lightweight and beautifully designed floating dock built to give you instant access to your favorite apps, games, files, and folders without cluttering your taskbar.

Combining the fluid elegance of macOS docks with the modern **Windows 11 Fluent glassmorphic aesthetic**, LaunchDock keeps your screen clean and your workflow fast.

---

### ✨ Key Features:

* 💎 **Stunning Glassmorphic Design:** Gorgeous frosted acrylic background, smooth animations, and reactive specular glow that naturally blends into your wallpaper.
* 🚀 **Instant Drag-and-Drop:** Simply drag any app, `.exe`, file, folder, or shortcut directly onto the dock to pin it in seconds.
* ⚡ **Lightning-Fast App Search:** Type to instantly filter and launch apps in milliseconds without reaching for the Windows Start menu.
* 🎨 **Deep Customization:**
  * Dark & Light themes with customizable accent colors.
  * Fine-tune window opacity, item background intensity, and blur levels.
  * Adjust dock width, icon dimensions, and hover animation speeds.
* 📌 **Stay on Top or Float Minimized:** Keep your dock always visible or let it auto-collapse cleanly when not in use.
* 🛡️ **100% Private & Lightweight:** Completely offline-first with zero tracking, no accounts required, and minimal memory usage.

---

### 💡 Perfect For:
* **Desktop Minimalists:** Keep your desktop wallpaper pristine while maintaining quick access to everything you use.
* **Gamers & Streamers:** Launch games, mods, overlays, and communication tools with zero distraction.
* **Power Users & Creatives:** Quickly switch between IDEs, editing software, browser profiles, and project folders.
