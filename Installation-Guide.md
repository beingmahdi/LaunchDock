# LaunchDock — Installation Guide

This quick step-by-step guide will walk you through installing and launching **LaunchDock** on your Windows PC.

---

## 📋 System Check

Before beginning, ensure your computer meets the following minimum requirements:
- **Operating System**: Windows 10 or Windows 11 (64-bit)
- **Available Space**: At least 250 MB free disk space

No additional software or runtime (such as Node.js, Python, or developer tools) is required. LaunchDock is fully standalone.

---

## 📥 Step 1: Run the Installer

1. Locate the downloaded file: **`LaunchDock-Setup.exe`**.
2. Double-click **`LaunchDock-Setup.exe`** to begin.

---

## 🛡️ Step 2: If Windows SmartScreen Appears

Because LaunchDock is an independent community application distributed without a commercial corporate certificate, Windows Defender SmartScreen may show a security notice:

1. When you see the blue window with **"Windows protected your PC"**:
2. Click the small link labeled **"More info"** (under the description text).
3. A button labeled **"Run anyway"** will appear in the bottom-right corner.
4. Click **"Run anyway"** to continue with the setup.

> *Note: This notice is standard for open-source Windows utilities and is safe to bypass.*

---

## ⚙️ Step 3: Choose Your Options

The LaunchDock installer window will appear:
- **Destination Folder**: By default, LaunchDock installs into your personal user programs folder (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\LaunchDock`). You can keep this default or click **Browse...** to select another folder.
- **Desktop Shortcut**: Check the **"Create Desktop Shortcut"** box if you would like an icon on your desktop.
- Click the violet **"Install LaunchDock"** button.

---

## ⏳ Step 4: Installation & Showcase

The installer will extract and configure LaunchDock on your system. During the quick installation process, you can preview the feature highlights on screen.

When the progress bar reaches 100%, you will see the **"Installation Complete!"** screen.

---

## 🚀 Step 5: Start Using LaunchDock

1. Click **"Launch LaunchDock"** (or open **LaunchDock** from your Start Menu / Desktop).
2. Look at the **top center of your primary screen**: you will see a subtle, rounded pill.
3. **Move your mouse pointer over the pill** — your new LaunchDock will glide open smoothly.
4. Drag any application or file from your computer and drop it into the dock to get started!

---

## 🔄 Updating or Reinstalling

To update LaunchDock in the future, simply run the new `LaunchDock-Setup.exe`. Your pinned applications, customized categories, and settings are saved separately and will remain intact automatically.
